## 1. Executive Summary

Divya Gyan is an AI-powered conversational chatbot trained on the wisdom of world mythological texts — including the Bhagavad Gita, Mahabharata, Ramayana, Upanishads, Arthashastra, Greek mythology, Buddhist Jataka tales, Stoic philosophy, and other ancient scriptures. The platform acts as a personal life consultant that responds to real-world human dilemmas with grounded, spiritually-informed, and practically actionable guidance.

> *"Ancient wisdom. Modern clarity. Life solutions rooted in timeless truth."*

Unlike generic AI assistants, Divya Gyan bridges the gap between ancient philosophical knowledge and contemporary life challenges — providing optimistic, realistic, and morally grounded advice that feels both divine and deeply human.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Modern individuals frequently face complex life situations — relationship conflicts, career crossroads, ethical dilemmas, grief, purpose crises, and more. While therapists and counsellors exist, they are expensive, time-constrained, and not always accessible. Generic AI chatbots offer surface-level, generic advice.

Meanwhile, ancient mythological and philosophical texts contain extraordinarily nuanced, empathetic, and battle-tested wisdom about human nature and life — yet they remain inaccessible to the average person because of language barriers, complexity, and lack of contextual guidance.

There is a clear, unmet need for a platform that:

- Makes timeless ancient wisdom accessible and conversational
- Provides personalised, situation-aware life guidance
- Delivers responses that are optimistic, non-judgmental, and action-oriented
- Feels like speaking to a wise sage — not a robot

## 6. Core Features & Functional Requirements

### 6.1 Conversational Life Consultation

- Natural language chat interface for users to describe their life situations
- AI listens, empathises, and asks clarifying questions before responding
- Generates multi-layered responses: emotional acknowledgment → mythological insight → practical advice → life lesson
- Supports long-form conversations with context memory within a session

### 6.2 Response Framework (The 4-Layer Answer Model)

| **Layer** | **Component**              | **Description**                                          |
|-----------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| 1         | Empathetic Acknowledgment  | AI recognises the user's emotional state without judgment |
| 2         | Mythological Parallel      | Draws a relevant story or teaching from ancient texts    |
| 3         | Practical Guidance         | Translates ancient wisdom into concrete, modern action steps |
| 4         | Life Lesson & Affirmation  | Closes with an optimistic takeaway and motivational insight |

### 6.3 Situation Categories Handled

- Career & professional decisions
- Relationships (love, family, friendships, conflicts)
- Grief, loss, and emotional healing
- Purpose, identity, and existential questions
- Ethical dilemmas and moral confusion
- Stress, anxiety, and mental overwhelm
- Financial hardship and material struggles
- Leadership challenges

### 6.4 Personalisation Engine

- User profile: name, age, cultural background, preferred tradition (Hindu/Buddhist/Greek/Universal)
- Conversation history for returning users (cross-session memory)
- Adaptive tone: casual, devotional, philosophical, or practical based on user preference

### 6.5 Scripture Explorer

- Users can browse and search mythological texts by theme, emotion, or keyword
- AI summarises and explains any shloka, verse, or parable in plain language
- Daily wisdom: a curated quote or teaching each day

### 6.6 Guided Reflection Journeys

- 7-day and 21-day thematic programmes (e.g. 'Overcoming Fear', 'Finding Purpose')
- Each day includes a scripture reading, reflection prompt, and AI guidance session

## 8. AI Model Architecture

### 8.1 Training Approach

- Base LLM (e.g. GPT-4 / Claude / Llama) fine-tuned on curated mythological datasets
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for accurate scripture citation
- Custom prompt engineering for the 4-layer response framework
- RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) using expert reviewers — scholars, therapists, spiritual practitioners

### 8.2 Data Pipeline

- Source texts digitised, translated, and annotated by subject-matter experts
- Thematic tagging: each passage tagged with emotions, life situations, and lesson categories
- Continuous dataset expansion with new texts post-launch

### 8.3 Safety Guardrails

- Automatic detection of crisis signals (suicidal ideation, abuse, extreme distress) → redirect to professional resources
- Refusal to provide medical, legal, or financial advice — always recommend professionals
- Content moderation layer for harmful or offensive inputs

## 13. Risks & Mitigations

| **Risk**                           | **Impact** | **Mitigation Strategy**                                                  |
|------------------------------------|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Misinterpretation of sacred texts  | High       | Expert panel review; multiple scholarly validations before launch        |
| Religious / cultural sensitivity   | High       | Universal, non-denominational framing; optional tradition preference     |
| AI hallucination of fake quotes    | High       | RAG pipeline ensures citation accuracy; human audit layer                |
| Crisis situations mishandled       | Critical   | Mandatory safety guardrails + professional referral system               |
| User over-reliance on AI           | Medium     | Regular reminders that AI supplements, not replaces, human support       |
| Competitive imitation              | Medium     | Build proprietary dataset + deep community moat                          |
